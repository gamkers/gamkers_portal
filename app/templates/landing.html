About Gamkers

More than a community. An ecosystem for builders.

Gamkers is a community-driven cybersecurity and technology education initiative dedicated to making advanced technology accessible through practical learning, hands-on experiences and collaborative innovation — bringing together students, enthusiasts, developers, researchers and professionals.

“We believe cybersecurity cannot be mastered through theory alone. It requires experimentation, problem-solving, collaboration and real-world exposure.”

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about joining, events and contributing.

Hands-on, every time.Real tools, real devices, guided labs.
{{ ic('mail') }}
Still have a question?gamkers@gmail.com
Who can join Gamkers?{{ ic('plus') }}

Anyone curious about cybersecurity and technology — students taking their first step, developers exploring new domains, and professionals who want to share knowledge.

Do I need prior experience?{{ ic('plus') }}

No. Sessions range from fundamentals to advanced topics, and the approach is hands-on: you learn by experimenting with real tools, devices and projects in a guided environment.

How do I register for an event?{{ ic('plus') }}

Open any event in the Events section. Each event has its own page with the date, time, venue and full description — fill in the registration form there.

Can my college host a Gamkers workshop?{{ ic('plus') }}

Yes. We collaborate with colleges on workshops, technical talks and hands-on sessions, including hardware and RF security demos. Reach us at gamkers@gmail.com.

How can I become a contributor?{{ ic('plus') }}

Join our Discord and get involved in research, content, hardware projects or community events.

Where can I follow Gamkers?{{ ic('plus') }}

Instagram @gamkers, YouTube Gamkers, and our Discord community.
