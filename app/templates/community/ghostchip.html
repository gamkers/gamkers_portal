GhostChip

The Ultimate ESP32-S3 USB Development Platform

GhostChip is the world's first BadUSB device with built-in AI script generation. Describe your payload in plain English — the AI writes, optimizes, and executes the DuckyScript automatically. Plug into any USB port and you're operational in seconds.

Comes with a full companion app (Android APK + Web PWA) featuring a script editor, payload simulator, Wi-Fi/BLE scanner, live keyboard, NeoPixel control, and OTA firmware updates.
